Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Holy Bible Online handles information when you visit the website or choose to sign in with Google. It is written for the current website features. If new features collect or store additional information, this policy should be updated before those features are launched.

1. Information we may receive

If you choose to sign in with Google, the authentication service may provide basic profile information that you authorize, such as your name, email address, and profile picture. You can review Google's account chooser and consent prompts before signing in.

We do not ask for access to your Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts, or other Google account content as part of basic sign-in.

When you visit the website, your browser and hosting provider may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure the site, such as IP address, browser type, device information, and request logs. The exact information and retention period depend on the providers' systems.

2. How information is used

Information received through Google sign-in is used to authenticate you, maintain your signed-in session, and display your account details within the website. Technical information may be used by hosting and security services to operate, troubleshoot, and protect the website.

3. Service providers

Holy Bible Online is hosted using GitHub Pages and uses Firebase Authentication, a service provided by Google, for Google sign-in. These providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • GitHub Privacy Statement

4. Cookies and browser storage

Authentication services may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies to complete sign-in and remember your session. Your browser settings can affect these technologies. Blocking them may prevent some sign-in features from working.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate the website, or disclosed when required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect the website, its users, or others.

6. Data retention and deletion requests

Authentication information may remain with Firebase and Google for as long as needed to provide the sign-in service and comply with applicable requirements. If you want to ask about access, correction, or deletion of information associated with your use of this website, contact us at jaymolina768@gmail.com. We will review the request and explain any steps that must be completed through Google or Firebase.

7. Children's privacy

Holy Bible Online is a general-audience Bible reading website and is not specifically directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information and you would like it reviewed, contact us using the email below.

8. Security

We rely on the security features provided by our hosting and authentication providers and take reasonable care when maintaining the website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website's features or practices change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
