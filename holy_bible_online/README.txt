THE HOLY BIBLE ONLINE - UPDATED DESIGN

Files:
- index.html: page layout, top Bible branding, scripture search, random verse, book list
- style.css: responsive dark-blue design
- script.js: scripture reference lookup, topic search, random inspirational verse, book/chapter reader
- assets/open-bible.png: Bible artwork from your reference image

Run:
1. Extract the ZIP.
2. Open the folder in VS Code.
3. Use Live Server, or place it in C:\xampp\htdocs\holy_bible_online and visit http://localhost/holy_bible_online/

Search:
- Bible references such as John 3:16 or Psalm 23 load through bible-api.com.
- Topic search searches the included inspirational verse collection (topics include hope, love, strength, prayer, fear, peace, courage, trust, and more).
- The random inspirational verse changes when you press New random verse.
An internet connection is required for live Bible passage lookup.
